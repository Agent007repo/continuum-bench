# Third-party material

No external model weights, datasets, paper PDFs or legal excerpts are included. Original synthetic examples are labelled in `data/DATA_CARD.md`. External pages are linked and paraphrased briefly in the source register. We have not verified EUR-Lex reuse terms, so importing legal excerpts is disabled.

Model and dataset licences must be recorded with revision and checksum before downloads enter a research run. Each locked software dependency retains its own licence; `scripts/dependency_licenses.py` emits installed metadata. A missing or ambiguous licence blocks final release until reviewed. The licences of our original code and documentation do not relicense dependencies or external texts.

The official ISO page confirms the edition and number of characteristics, but the complete standard was not acquired. Specific characteristic labels remain unverified. We distribute neither the standard nor an invented taxonomy.
