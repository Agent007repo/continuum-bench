# Related work and verification register

We distinguish reading an abstract from reviewing a complete paper. `sources.json` records which primary pages were opened, the narrow claim supported, and the retrieval date. Failed retrievals support no external claim.

For TrustTrace, we use Razavian's design-reasoning profile and the Ethics-Aware DecidArch abstract to motivate explicit reasoning about stakeholder disagreements. We do not claim that our checklist reproduces that study's validated intervention.

For ContinuumBench, the f-inference public objective motivates cloud/edge/device placement. Ding's roadmap and survey are verified reading targets. Oliveira's access-control and unlearning-verification abstracts motivate auditable boundaries. We infer portfolio relevance; supervisors have not reviewed or endorsed these projects.

The final report requires at least 25 verified references per project. This milestone does not meet that criterion and contains no invented filler citations. Full-text reading, claim-level synthesis, licence checking and identifier resolution are tracked in `STATUS.md`.
